Police-recorded crime by local authority

Crimes and anti-social behaviour recorded by the police in the area, per 1,000 residents, over the 12 months from September 2025 to August 2026. England and Wales; no figures for Greater Manchester, whose force does not publish street-level data. Gloucestershire's figures are from September 2025 to January 2026 only, scaled up to a year. City centres score high because many crimes happen where people work, shop and go out, not where they live.
